How to use the lab

Choose an operation in Expression workspace. Press and hold an operation to open exercise settings, or use the horizontal Tools ribbon to build and edit tiles. Drag tiles onto each other to apply the selected operation. Or click two tiles to select them, then choose Apply selected pair.

  • Add/Subtract: like monomials combine by adding or subtracting signed coefficients; unlike terms stay separate.
  • Multiply: multiply coefficients and add exponents. Divide: divide coefficients and subtract exponents. Signs follow the usual sign rules.
  • Tile options: right-click a tile (or press and hold on touch) to edit it. Wrap a tile in parentheses, brackets, braces, or angle brackets; drag another tile into the group, or drag a tile out. An empty group disappears. The − sign tile reverses a tile sign; applying it to a group flips every sign and opens the grouping.
  • Comparison board: open Board to divide the workspace into one, two or three sections; change each comparison sign directly on the divider. Select a side before creating a tile.
  • Use Board for dots, grids, lines, sections, temporary containers, vivid tile colors, theme, organization and the expression monitor. Toggle Written process to show the operation log. Expand Math Toolkit only when you need its fraction and factor tools.

Keyboard: Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+S redo · Ctrl+C copy · Ctrl+V paste · Ctrl+E select all · Delete remove selected.
